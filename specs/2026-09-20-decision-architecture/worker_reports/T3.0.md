## Verification

`requirements.md` §"Freshness Protocol" contains:

- Hash definition formula:
  ```
  stateHash = sha256(issue.updatedAt || '|' || comments.length || '|' || lastReceiptSha || '|' || factory.lastTriageAt || '|' || issue.labels.join(','))
  ```
- Staleness threshold rule: cached judgment reused when `noul_yes < 0.2`; full batch triggered otherwise.
- Configurable knob in `decisions.yaml` under `decisions.freshness.threshold`.
- Skip rule: orchestrator logs `judgment.skip` with reason `state_unchanged`; the decision is never silently elided.
