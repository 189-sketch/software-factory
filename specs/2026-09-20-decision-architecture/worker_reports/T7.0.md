### Notable implementation notes

- `extractSection(text, headerRe)` walks the markdown character-by-character
  while tracking fenced code-block state. This avoids misreading `# comment`
  lines inside YAML / TS examples as markdown headings (a real bug caught
  during initial testing).
- The `lineage` check resolves every `file:line` reference in
  `requirements.md` against the current HEAD; `src/agents/triage.ts:182`,
  `src/agents/review-pr.ts:90`, `src/agents/verify-behavior.ts:119`, and
  `src/agents/triage.ts:254` all resolve at HEAD `7019dc6`.
- The `out-of-scope` check recognises markdown-bold phase tags
  (`**Phase B**`, `**Phase C**`, `**Phase D**`) plus the two implicit-defer
  phrases ("Phase A is purely additive", "are referenced but not edited").
- The `roadmap-changelog` check enforces both files; it will only pass
  after T7.1 lands.
